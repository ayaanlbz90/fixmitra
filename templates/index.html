FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to pay before the repair starts? +
No. FixMitra bookings are pay-after-service — the final cost is confirmed only after diagnosis and the repair is complete.
Is home repair available everywhere? +
Home visits currently cover select cities. Outside those areas, choose Pickup & Repair — available pan-India with safe transit cover.
What warranty do I get? +
Every repair comes with a technician-backed warranty of up to 90 days, shown on your booking and receipt.
How do I track my repair? +
Use the Track Repair page with your booking code (e.g. FM-123456) to see live status and technician assignment.
